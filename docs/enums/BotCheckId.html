    Enumeration BotCheckId

    The client-side bot signals evaluated by https://deviceandbrowserinfo.com/are_you_a_bot, ported here, plus the navigator.vendor and Apple Pay consistency checks that FingerprintJS collects as entropy sources. Each is a faithful reimplementation of the documented check (their source is not public). The verdict is derived entirely in the browser: no IP reputation, no behavioral data.
